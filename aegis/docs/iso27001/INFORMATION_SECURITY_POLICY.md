# Information security policy (draft)

**Organization:** Nexus AI Assurance
**Approved by:** ____________________ (Founder and CEO)  **Date:** ____________
**Review:** at least once a year and after any major change

## Purpose

Nexus AI Assurance builds AEGIS so that organizations can trust what their AI agents do. Customers
can only trust AEGIS if the company protects its own information and theirs to the same standard.

## Commitments

1. **Protect confidentiality, integrity and availability** of company and customer information
   according to its classification and the risks it faces.
2. **Meet legal, regulatory and contractual requirements,** including UAE data protection law and
   customer security requirements.
3. **Manage risk deliberately.** Risks are recorded, owned, treated and reviewed. Residual risks are
   accepted only by top management.
4. **Build securely.** Every change to AEGIS is version-controlled, tested and scanned before release.
   Secrets never enter source code. Production fails closed.
5. **Use synthetic data** for development, testing and demonstrations.
6. **Respond to incidents** promptly, inform affected customers and authorities as required, and learn
   from each one.
7. **Improve continually,** through monitoring, internal audit, independent testing and management
   review.

## Objectives (reviewed each management review)

- No release of AEGIS with a known critical or high vulnerability that has an available fix
- No secrets or customer data in source control
- Every customer-reported security issue acknowledged within one business day
- Penetration test completed before the first production deployment for a customer, and yearly after
- All "To do" items in the Statement of Applicability closed before the certification audit

## Responsibilities

Top management owns this policy and provides resources. The security lead runs the management
system. Everyone working for the company follows the topic policies in `TOPIC_POLICIES.md` and reports
suspected incidents immediately. Breaches of policy are handled under the disciplinary terms of each
person's agreement.
