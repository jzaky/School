## 1. Approach

- **Training follows the rollout.** Each group is trained just before the phase that affects them goes live (see the Implementation Plan), so people use what they learn within days.
- **Practise first, then go live.** Every session uses the public demonstration school, Horizon International School Dubai, which has fictional staff, students and families, months of realistic history in English and Arabic, and resets every night. Nobody can break anything there. Real Al Durrah data is never entered in the demonstration school.
- **Then do it for real.** After practice, each person completes their first real tasks in Al Durrah's own Horizon school, with the trainer present or on call.
- **Train the trainers.** The school names champions who learn in more depth and then train colleagues, so the school does not depend on the Supplier for every new member of staff.
- **Both languages.** Sessions are delivered in English, with Arabic where the audience prefers it. Quick reference cards and videos are in English and Arabic.
- **Short and hands-on.** Most sessions are 45 to 90 minutes, with at least half the time spent doing tasks.

### Before each session

- Each participant has a laptop (or a phone for the parents' session) with a current browser.
- The trainer presses **Reset demo** in the demonstration school so everyone starts from the same data.
- For practice in Al Durrah's own school, participants have accepted their invitation and can sign in with the agreed method.

## 3. Sessions in detail

### Session 1: Kickoff (60 minutes)

**Audience:** Principal, school administrator, Head of IT, Designated Safeguarding Lead, head of counseling, career advisor, registrar, finance officer, admissions officer, data protection lead. Supplier account lead and implementation lead.

**Agenda:**
1. Goals and what success looks like for Al Durrah (10 min).
2. The three phases and the dates (10 min).
3. Who does what: named people on both sides (10 min).
4. Decisions needed now (20 min): sign-in method (Google, Microsoft, ClassLink or password), AI drafting on or off, WhatsApp now or later, identity document policy, the five launch services for Phase 2, the champions.
5. The data request and how data will be moved (5 min).
6. Next steps and the weekly check-in (5 min).

**What they see in the demonstration school:** a 10-minute tour: Rania Nasser's parent home in Arabic, Adam Nasser's university planning, Khalid Yousef's restricted safeguarding queue, and Omar Al Mansoori's analytics.

**Outcome:** decisions recorded, names confirmed, data request sent.

### Session 2: Admin setup session (90 minutes)

**Audience:** school administrator, Head of IT, registrar.

**Agenda** (in Al Durrah's own Horizon school, the school administrator drives):
1. Sign up and confirm the email (10 min).
2. Profile: names in English and Arabic, logo, colours, school days, time zone, SPEA as regulator (15 min).
3. Academic year: terms, holidays, bell schedule (10 min).
4. Import center: staff, then students and guardians, then classes and enrolments. Fix the rows the preview flags, then import again (25 min).
5. Roles and access: assign the Designated Safeguarding Lead and deputy, registrar, counselors, career advisor, heads of department; run Access check on one teacher (15 min).
6. Invitations: how staff and families will join (15 min).

**Outcome:** a working Al Durrah school with Phase 1 people loaded.

### Session 3: School administrator deep dive (2 x 90 minutes)

**Audience:** school administrator and a deputy.

**Part A: services and communication**
- Services and forms: edit a form, add a field with Arabic and English labels, conditional questions, preview and publish.
- Workflows: open the visual route for a service, change an approver, validate, run the dry run, publish.
- Document templates: letter wording, merge fields, logo and seal, preview the PDF in English and Arabic.
- Notification message texts.
- Invitations, family join code and poster, join requests.

**Part B: oversight and compliance**
- Analytics and pilot measures for a chosen period, CSV download.
- Compliance: processing purposes and consent, retention periods, approved processors, AI switches, identity document policy, data subject request log, person data export and erasure preview, breach log.
- Audit log: filter and export.
- Inspection evidence pack for a term.
- School data export (what it contains and who can run it).

**Practise in the demonstration school as Aisha Rahman:** change the parent meeting form, simulate the subject change workflow, preview the enrolment letter, export the audit log for a week, preview (do not run) an erasure.

**Outcome:** the administrator can change forms, routes and letters without the Supplier.

### Session 4: Head of IT technical session (2 hours)

**Audience:** Head of IT and IT team.

**Agenda:**
1. Security Overview walkthrough: tenant isolation, roles, sensitive records, encryption, audit, backups, known gaps (30 min).
2. Sign-in: Google and Microsoft set-up, the staff email domain rule, what happens with ClassLink, how to handle a lost account (20 min).
3. Roles and access: sensitive permissions, Access check, removing leavers, guardrails (15 min).
4. Integrations: create a school API key with scopes, the API guide and OpenAPI file, scheduled sync from the current system with column mapping and run history (30 min).
5. Compliance switches, approved processors, the data export (15 min).
6. Devices and browsers, the installable app, phone alerts, email sending domain, network allow lists (10 min).

**Practise in the demonstration school as Aisha Rahman:** Access check on Daniel Carter; create and revoke an API key; open the scheduled sync source and its run history; export the audit log.

**Outcome:** the Head of IT can run sign-in, accounts, integrations and the audit log, and knows how to raise and escalate incidents.

### Session 5: Counselors and career advisor (2 x 90 minutes)

**Audience:** counselors, career advisor, head of high school.

**Part A: university guidance and course selection (Phase 1)**
- University planning: programme search with filters, compare, requirements from official university pages with the source quote, what "official" and "verified" mean, Mark verified.
- Subject levels and AP guidance: which targets need AP, which accept a regular course.
- Course selection for next year: what students and parents see, "Needed for N of your targets", registering on behalf, demand statistics.
- Counselor dashboard and transcript import with mapping review.
- Application tracker: pipeline board, deadlines and tasks, teacher recommendation letter requests.
- Career assessment and matches: review and approve before families see them; the career report PDF.
- University fairs and visits; partner resources.

**Part B: cases and meetings (Phase 2)**
- Caseload, referrals from teachers, versioned case notes, tasks, action plans.
- Meetings: booking with families, rescheduling, reminders and calendar files. (Meeting types and staff availability are set up for the school by the Supplier from the list the school provides; there is no self-service screen for them yet.)
- AI drafts: a pre-meeting brief and an action plan draft, and why every draft must be reviewed.
- Wellbeing cases: who can see them, recording a decision to inform the family with a reason.

**Practise in the demonstration school:** as Layla Hassan, review Adam Nasser's matches and plan; as Adam, check how a course choice changes his university options; as Sarah Ahmed, add a case note, amend it and see the version history, then book a meeting with Rania Nasser.

**Outcome:** counselors use Horizon for the next course selection round and for Grade 12 applications.

### Session 7: Students' orientation (30 minutes, Phase 1)

**Practise in the demonstration school as Adam Nasser,** then do the real course selection in Al Durrah's school.

### Session 8 and Session 13: Parents' orientation (30 minutes, Phase 1 and Phase 2)

**Audience:** parents. Session 8 is for parents of Grades 9 to 12 in Phase 1; Session 13 is for all parents in Phase 2.

**Format:** delivered in English and in Arabic (separately or bilingual), online and in person, and recorded for parents who cannot attend.

**Agenda:**
1. Joining: the invitation email or the family join code on the poster; adding each child with the student number and date of birth; what happens if details do not match (5 min).
2. Installing Horizon on a phone home screen and turning on alerts; WhatsApp opt-in if the school offers it (5 min).
3. Phase 1: your child's course choices and university planning; the career report; registering for university fairs (5 min).
4. Phase 2: requesting a school letter; reporting an absence and who sees the reason; booking a parent meeting; messages with staff on a request (10 min).
5. Privacy in one minute: what staff can see, what is never shared automatically, where to read the privacy notice (3 min).
6. Questions (2 min).

**Practise:** parents watch the trainer as Rania Nasser in Arabic, then do it on their own phone in Al Durrah's school.

### Session 9: Train the trainer (2 hours, plus follow-up)

**Audience:** champions: [one per section: KG, elementary, middle, high school], plus one from the registrar's office and one counselor. Names: [champion names].

**Agenda:**
1. Deeper practice of every teacher and parent task (45 min).

### Session 10: Safeguarding lead and deputy (90 minutes, confidential)

**Audience:** Designated Safeguarding Lead, deputy, [wellbeing lead]. No other staff.

**Agenda:**
1. How a concern arrives: the safeguarding concern form, concern levels, and immediate danger alerts (15 min).
2. The restricted queue, the case page, the chronology, notes that can be amended but never deleted (20 min).
3. External referrals (for example to a Child Protection Unit) and their reference numbers (10 min).
4. Deciding whether to inform the family, and recording the reason (10 min).
5. Access: access grants, time-limited emergency (break-glass) access and the alert it sends, the audit tab showing every view (15 min).
6. Chronology export, retention (review only, never deleted automatically) and what happens at exit (10 min).
7. Check together that Al Durrah's safeguarding workflow routes to the right people (10 min).

**Practise in the demonstration school:** as Daniel Carter, raise a concern and see that only "being handled" is visible; as Khalid Yousef, open it, add a note, record an external referral, record a decision not to inform the family with a reason, view the audit tab; as Omar Al Mansoori, use emergency access and see the alert reach Khalid.

**Outcome:** the DSL confirms the safeguarding route and access in Al Durrah's school are correct before Phase 2 go-live.

**Practise in the demonstration school:** as Rania Nasser, report a sick day with a note; as the Registrar, approve it; as Daniel Carter, see "Excused absence, reported by parent" without the reason.

**Phase 3 add-on (before parallel running):** taking the daily register, attendance reports, and the government reporting exports, once those modules are delivered.

## 6. Adoption checklist: 30, 60 and 90 days

Day 0 is the Phase 1 go-live. Figures come from Horizon's Analytics page (Pilot measures and Service levels), which excludes wellbeing and safeguarding records by design. Targets are agreed at kickoff.

### By day 30 (end of Phase 1)

- [ ] All staff in Phase 1 scope have signed in at least once ([target] percent).
- [ ] Counselors and career advisor use Horizon for every Grade 12 application and for course selection.
- [ ] Next year's offerings, with AP flags, are set up and the course selection window is open or scheduled.
- [ ] Parents of Grades 9 to 12 joined: [target] percent of families.
- [ ] Requirements for the [50 to 100] most-targeted programmes checked by counselors.
- [ ] Champions named, trained and signed off.
- [ ] Phase 1 quick reference cards and videos 3 to 7 shared.
- [ ] First monthly support report reviewed.
- [ ] Phase 1 acceptance signed.

### By day 60 (Phase 2 live)

- [ ] DSL and deputy trained; the safeguarding route tested and confirmed in writing by the DSL.
- [ ] All teaching staff trained (Session 12) and active ([target] percent staff active in the last 30 days).
- [ ] Five launch services live with Al Durrah's forms, routes and letters.
- [ ] Parents across the whole school joined: [target] percent of families.
- [ ] Absence reports come through Horizon, with reasons visible only to the right people.
- [ ] Share of requests closed within target: [target] percent.
- [ ] Champions run the monthly refresher without the Supplier.
- [ ] Training attendance sheets kept by the school (Horizon does not record training attendance).

### By day 90 (review and Phase 3 readiness)

- [ ] Review meeting with the Principal: results against each target, feedback from staff and parents.
- [ ] New staff onboarding uses the cards and videos, with a champion as buddy.
- [ ] Phase 3 decisions taken: the term boundary for parallel running, the readiness of attendance, admissions, fees and government reporting, the data to migrate.
- [ ] Phase 3 training (Sessions 14, 15 and 16) scheduled after module acceptance.
- [ ] Phase 2 acceptance signed.
