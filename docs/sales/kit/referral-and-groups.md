## Part 2: One-page pitch for school groups

### Horizon for school groups

**One platform, every school in the group, each school's data kept apart.**

Every school in the group gets its own Horizon tenant: its own staff, families, records and settings, separated from the other schools by database row level security. Group leaders get consistency without any school seeing another school's records.

### What a group gets today

- **One setup per school, the same starting point.** Every new school starts from the same complete configuration: services, forms, approval routes, bilingual letters, message templates, roles, UAE calendar. Each school then runs one 90-minute setup and imports its own staff and families.
- **One sign-in for people who work across schools.** A person invited to several schools switches between them with the school switcher, with the right role in each.
- **Each school keeps its own identity.** Logo, colours, names, letterhead and seal on every letter and report card.
- **Group-wide safeguards by design.** In every school, wellbeing and safeguarding records never appear in dashboards or analytics, and every view of a sensitive record is audited.
- **Comparable figures.** Every school's Analytics page reports the same measures (requests, time to close, share within target, meetings held), so results can be compared school by school.

### Planned for groups

- **Shared templates:** publish a form, approval route or letter once and copy it to every school in the group.
- **Group dashboard:** the same operational figures across all schools on one page, with wellbeing and safeguarding data excluded, as in every Horizon dashboard.
