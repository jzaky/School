# Talking about ROI

Rule one: never quote a saving the school has not confirmed. Every number below is an assumption the school fills in, and the pilot then measures what actually happened.

## 1. Where the time goes today

Ask the school to estimate each line in week 1 (a quick count over one normal week is enough).

| Category | What staff do today | What Horizon changes | Assumption to confirm |
|---|---|---|---|
| School documents and letters | Take the request by phone or at reception, check details, type the letter, get a signature, call the parent | Parent requests online; the letter is generated in English and Arabic from a template; approval with a signature in one inbox; parent notified | [minutes per letter today], [letters per month] |
| Absence reports | Answer calls and emails, write them down, pass them on | Parent reports online; it goes to the right person with a 24-hour target | [minutes per absence], [absences reported per month] |
| Meeting booking | Emails back and forth to find a time | Parent picks a free slot; confirmation and reminders are automatic | [minutes per meeting booked], [meetings per month] |
| Teacher referrals | Emails or paper forms, chasing who has it | One form, routed to the right person, tracked to closure | [minutes per referral], [referrals per month] |
| Safeguarding records | Notes in several places; assembling a chronology by hand | One restricted record with the chronology built as you go, exportable by the DSL | [hours per chronology], [cases per term] |
| "Where is my request?" calls | Reception looks it up and calls back | Families see each step themselves | [calls per week], [minutes per call] |
| Data entry at the start of a year | Typing staff and student lists into new tools | Import center from existing spreadsheet exports | [hours today] |

Staff cost per hour: [the school's figure, or leave out and talk in hours].

The public /pricing page has the same kind of estimate as a calculator (letters, absence reports, meeting booking and parent messages, with staff cost per hour). Every assumption is labelled and editable; use the school's own figures, and say it is an estimate, not a promise.

## 2. How to work it out

For each category:

> hours saved per month = (minutes today minus minutes with Horizon) x volume per month / 60

Take "minutes with Horizon" from the pilot, not from us. Talk in staff hours first; money only if the school wants it, with its own hourly cost.

Beyond time, name the benefits the school cares about, without putting a number on them unless the school does:

- Fewer missed targets (share of requests closed within target).
- A clear record for inspections and audits (audit log, recorded decisions).
- Safeguarding handled the same way every time.
- A better experience for families in Arabic and English.

## 3. How the pilot measures it

| What | Where in Horizon |
|---|---|
| Volume per service | Analytics: Requests by service (last 7, 30 or 90 days) |
| Time to close | Analytics: Average time to close; Slowest services (average hours per service); Time to close, week by week |
| Share within target | Analytics: Service levels, Within target |
| Letters issued | Analytics: Guidance and letters, Letters issued |
| Meetings held as booked | Analytics: Service levels, Meetings held |
| Staff actually using it | Analytics: Pilot measures, Staff active (any period, CSV download) |
| Parent adoption | Analytics: Pilot measures, Parent adoption |
| Median days to issue a letter | Analytics: Pilot measures |

Minutes per task with Horizon are not tracked by the app. Ask two or three staff to time a handful of tasks in week 6 and again at the end of term.

## 4. What to say in the meeting

- "We will not give you a savings figure. We will measure yours."
- "Tell us how long a letter takes today and how many you issue. At the review you will see the real number next to it."
- "If the agreed measures are not met, you do not pay the pilot fee."
