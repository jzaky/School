# Live demo script

About 20 minutes. Everything runs on the demo school, Horizon International School, Dubai. All people and records are fictional.

## Before you start (5 minutes before)

1. Open the deployed URL and go to `/demo`.
2. Enter as any persona and press **Reset demo** in the yellow bar. The reset takes under a minute and restores every record, with dates relative to today.
3. Open two browser windows side by side: one for the requester (student or parent), one for staff. Use a private window for the second, because each window holds one signed-in persona.
4. Optional: set the second window to Arabic with the language button to show RTL.

Tip: the **Guide** button (bottom corner) lists what to try for the current persona and ticks items off as you visit them.

## 1. Opening (2 minutes)

- Landing page: "One portal for every school procedure". Point out the five journeys and the trust section.
- Click **Explore the live demo**. Each card is a real account with its own permissions.

## 2. Career guidance (4 minutes) - Adam, then Layla

1. Enter as **Adam Nasser** (Student, Grade 9). Home shows his requests, meetings and tasks.
2. **Career** > **Take the assessment**. Answer quickly; pick high scores for technology and analysis questions.
3. Results: **AI Engineer** is among the top matches, with the reasons shown. Click **Choose this career**.
4. **Book the career advisor**: pick a free slot with Layla, then fill the short intake and submit. The request page shows each step.
5. Switch to **Layla Hassan** (Career Advisor). Open the new case from Home or Cases.
6. **Generate brief** (AI panel). Point out: "Draft. Review before use." and that no sensitive data is sent.
7. **Draft action plan**, edit an item, then **Approve and share**. Tasks are created for Adam and the family is updated.
8. Back as Adam: **Tasks** now shows the plan; the request is Completed.

## 3. Teacher referral (3 minutes) - Daniel, then Sarah

1. Enter as **Daniel Carter** (Teacher). **Services** > **Refer a student: academic concern** for Adam. Submit.
2. Switch to **Sarah Ahmed** (Counselor). The referral is in her caseload. Open the case: notes are versioned and can never be silently edited.
3. **Book a meeting** with Rania invited. Show the calendar week view in Arabic if time allows.
4. Draft an action plan and set a follow-up date. It appears on Sarah's calendar.

## 4. Official letter (2 minutes) - Rania, then the registrar

1. Enter as **Rania Nasser** (Parent). **Services** > **Request a school document**. Choose Adam, Enrollment letter, Bilingual, purpose Embassy.
2. Switch to **Registrar**. **Approvals** > approve with a signature.
3. Back as Rania: notification "Your document is ready". **Documents** > open the PDF. Show Arabic and English side by side with correct shaping.

## 5. Subject change (2 minutes) - Rania, Daniel, Head of Computing, Registrar

1. As Rania: **Subject change** for Adam, Physics to Computer Science.
2. The request page shows three approval steps. Approve as **Daniel**, then **Head of Computing**, then **Registrar** (use the persona switcher in the yellow bar).
3. Rania is notified that the timetable was updated.

## 6. Parent-teacher meeting (1 minute) - Rania

1. **Book a parent meeting**: pick Yara, then her teacher, then a free slot. Confirmation shows a calendar download (.ics).

## 7. Safeguarding (2 minutes) - Daniel, then Khalid

1. As Daniel: **Safeguarding** > raise a concern. After submitting, Daniel only sees "Received and being handled". He cannot read the case.
2. As **Khalid Yousef** (DSL): the restricted queue, the full chronology, the recorded decision about telling the family (never automatic), and the audit tab showing who viewed the record.

## 8. Leadership and compliance (2 minutes) - Omar, then Aisha

1. **Omar Al Mansoori** (Principal): Home KPIs, then **Analytics**. Ask "Which service is slowest?". Point out that wellbeing and safeguarding records are excluded.
2. **Aisha Rahman** (Admin): **Forms** (drag-and-drop builder with preview), **Workflows** (visual builder, validate, simulate), **Document templates** (live PDF preview), **Compliance** (AI safeguards, retention, consent, data requests), **Audit log**.

## Close

- Everything shown works in Arabic. Switch language on any page.
- Press **Reset demo** after the session.

## If something goes wrong

- Stuck state: press **Reset demo**.
- Login loop: go to `/demo` and pick the persona again.
- No free slots: move to the next week in the booking picker; slots respect school days, buffers and notice periods.
